Chapter III — 3.7 Evaluation Procedure

Add under “Evaluation Instrument”, after the paragraph describing the existing Google Forms questionnaire. If your current copy already contains “AI Testing and Observation”, use this wording there once.

The ISO/IEC 25010-based questionnaire remains the instrument for the respondent evaluation. A supplementary assessment maps documented unit and integration test results and researcher observations to selected ISO/IEC 25059:2023 quality characteristics: functional correctness, robustness, transparency, and user controllability (International Organization for Standardization [ISO] & International Electrotechnical Commission [IEC], 2023). These characteristics are identified in the existing web application testing table in Chapter IV, together with the relevant test conditions, expected behaviors, actual results, and statuses.

For each assessed case, the researchers compare the actual behavior with a predefined expected outcome and retain the test inputs, observer, execution date, system version, and supporting screenshots or logs. The record distinguishes live AI responses from simulated, cached, or unavailable responses. A behavior without a documented observation remains Not assessed. The AI-related findings are interpreted descriptively and are not combined with the questionnaire means. This procedure does not require another respondent questionnaire or an additional ISO/IEC 25059 rating scale.
